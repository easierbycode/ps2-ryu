HOW TO PLAY

  • Switch (CFW): copy ps2-ryu.nro to sd:/switch/ and launch from hbmenu (Atmosphère). Buttons map by position, so the left face button punches whatever it is labelled.
  • Emulator: open the ISO in PCSX2, or in the CMG launcher's PlayStation 2 screen (Play!).
  • Real hardware: load over USB/SMB with Open PS2 Loader, or burn to DVD-R (softmodded console).
  • Controls: d-pad move · UP jump · DOWN crouch · punches on left face / top face / R (light/medium/heavy) · kicks on bottom face / right face / R2 · shoryuken → ↓ → punch (stronger punch, higher rise) · all three punches or L = super shoryuken · all kicks on L2.
  • SNES-style pad: punches Y / X / R2, kicks B / A / R, super on L2, all kicks on L.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, ↑ jump, ↓ crouch, V/C/E punch, Z/X/R kick, Q super (or V+C+E), A all kicks.
  • This page: with a gamepad, ↑↓ picks a button and the bottom face button selects it — or try the shoryuken input on the logo.
